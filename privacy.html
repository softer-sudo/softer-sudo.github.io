Privacy policy

second-brain is a personal app used by its author only, with the author's own Google account.

  • It reads and writes events in the author's Google Calendar to schedule the author's tasks.
  • Calendar data is processed only on the author's own computer. It is not sent to anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
